About

I'm a biomedical engineering graduate student focused on device design that starts in the clinical setting rather than the lab. Over the past year, I've served as Clinical Liaison on a Biomedical Clinical Needs Finding project, observing cardiac procedures — including CABG and TAVR — alongside a cardiac surgeon to identify where operating room efficiency breaks down, and conceptualizing a device to address it.

That work sits at the center of how I approach engineering: talk to the people using the tools, watch the process closely, and let the constraints of the room — sterility, time pressure, the surgical team's attention — shape the design, not just the spec sheet.

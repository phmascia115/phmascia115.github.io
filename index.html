About

Hi! My name is Phil and I am a Biomedical Engineering M.Eng. student at Boston University, focused on device design that starts in the clinical setting. I am currently serving as Clinical Liaison on a Biomedical Clinical Needs Finding project team, observing cardiac procedures to identify operating room inefficiencies, and conceptualizing a device to address it.

I aspire to find career opportunities that enable me to improve patient care or quality of life using engineering device innovation. I am driven by my passion towards device design, and am excited to find a place in the medical device industry where I can genuinely make a difference.

With a background in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Virginia, I came to Boston University with a solid background in device innovation, biology, and critical thinking. I have a strong understanding of the bridge between technical engineering and patient-care, which fuels my passion to create impactful technology for future generations.

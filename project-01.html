Improving OR Efficiency in Cardiac Procedures

  • Clinical Observation
  • Needs Finding
  • OR Workflow Analysis

Overview

Our team is observing cardiac procedures at Boston Medical Center, spanning a multitude of procedures, such as Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement surgery, Coronary Artery Bypass Graft surgery, and Mitral Valve Repair.

The purpose is for our team to recognize workflow inefficiencies during cardiac surgery, specifically something that we can act on. We would like to conceptualize and prototype a device that can improve an inefficiency in the cardiac surgery room, and will continue to work on this project throughout the course of the school year.

Approach

We are in the early stages of clinical observations and problem identification. Some inefficiencies we've observed include: Difficulty suturing underneath the mitral valve during mitral valve repair surgery; Suture organization, as the surgeon often has many sutures in his hand at once which can lead to tangling and a longer procedure.

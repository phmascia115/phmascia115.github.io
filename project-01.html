Improving OR Efficiency in Cardiac Procedures

  • Clinical Observation
  • Needs finding
  • OR Workflow Analysis

Overview

Our team is currently observing cardiac procedures at Boston Medical Center, spanning minimally invasive procedures, like Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement surgery, to open-heart procedures, including Coronary Artery Bypass Graft surgeries.

The purpose is for our team to recognize workflow inefficiencies during cardiac surgery, specifically something that we can act on. Our goal is to conceptualize and prototype a device that can improve an inefficiency in the cardiac surgery room.

Approach

We are currently in the early stages of clinical observations and problem identification. Some inefficiencies we've observed include: Difficulty seeing, and therefore suturing within left ventricle for mitral valve repair surgery; Suture organization, as the surgeon often has many sutures in his hand at once.

Check back to see updates on problem statement and device conceptualization.
